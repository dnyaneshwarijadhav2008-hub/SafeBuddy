    You're Not Alone

    If someone is threatening you, following you, asking for private pictures, or making you feel unsafe:

    1. Stop replying.
    2. Move somewhere safe.
    3. Tell a trusted adult.
    4. Block and report the person.
    5. If there is immediate danger, contact local emergency services.
